# Math News 2026-3-25

## The complete picture for clique factors in randomly perturbed graphs <Badge type="tip" text="94" />

- **Tags:** `组合数学` `随机图` `图论` `极值图论` 

- **Source:** `ArXiv_Math_CO` | [阅读原文](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.22081)
