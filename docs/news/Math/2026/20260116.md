## Mixing times for the TASEP on the circle <Badge type="tip" text="94" />

- **Tags:** `概率论` `统计物理` `排列组合` `随机过程` 

- **Source:** `ArXiv_Math_PR` | [阅读原文](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.11896)

> [摘要]
