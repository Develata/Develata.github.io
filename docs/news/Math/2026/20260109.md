## Rigorous results for timelike Liouville field theory <Badge type="tip" text="94" />

- **Tags:** `概率论` `数学物理` `随机分析` `场论` 

- **Source:** `ArXiv_Math_PR` | [阅读原文](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.02348)

> [摘要]
