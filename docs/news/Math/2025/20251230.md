## Superquantile-Gibbs Relaxation for Minima-selection in Bilevel Optimization <Badge type="tip" text="94" />

- **Tags:** `优化` `双层优化` `随机梯度` `概率方法` 

- **Source:** `ArXiv_Math_OC` | [阅读原文](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.05991)

> [摘要]
