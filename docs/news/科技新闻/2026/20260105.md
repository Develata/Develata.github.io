## Corundum – open-source FPGA-based NIC and platform for in-network compute <Badge type="tip" text="94" />

- **Tags:** `FPGA` `NIC` `in-network compute` `Linux driver` `PCIe DMA` `TDMA` `IEEE 1588` `high-performance networking` 

- **Source:** `HackerNews` | [阅读原文](https://github.com/corundum/corundum)

> [摘要]  
Corundum是一款开源高性能FPGA网络接口卡，支持10G/25G/100G以太网与PCIe Gen3，具备数千个可独立控制的队列、高精度TDMA调度与IEEE 1588 PTP时间戳，适用于网络内计算与低延迟数据平面处理。
