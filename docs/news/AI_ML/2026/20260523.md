## Vega: Zero\-knowledge proofs for digital identity in the age of AI <Badge type="tip" text="68" />

- **Tags:** `AI安全` `隐私保护` `零知识证明` 

- **Source:** `Microsoft Research Blog` | [阅读原文](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/blog/vega-zero-knowledge-proofs-for-digital-identity-in-the-age-of-ai)
