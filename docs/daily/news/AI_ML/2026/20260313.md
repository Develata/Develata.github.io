## MXFP8 Training for MoEs: 1.3x training speedup vs BF16 for Llama4 Scout on GB200 cluster using TorchAO and TorchTitan <Badge type="tip" text="92" />

- **Tags:** `大模型` `MoE` `低精度训练` `MXFP8` `TorchAO` `GEMM优化` 

- **Source:** `Pytorch_Blog` | [阅读原文](https://pytorch.org/blog/mxfp8-training-for-moes-1-3x-training-speedup-vs-bf16-for-llama4-scout-on-gb200-cluster-using-torchao-and-torchtitan/)

> [摘要]  
通过在TorchAO中使用动态MXFP8分组GEMM实现Llama4 Scout训练速度提升30.2%，达到理论峰值的81%，且收敛性与bfloat16相当，显著提升大规模MoE模型训练效率。
